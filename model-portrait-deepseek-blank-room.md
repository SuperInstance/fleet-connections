# Model Portrait: The Blank White Room — DeepSeek vs Wesley

**Date:** 2026-08-12 22:25 AKDT
**Models:** DeepSeek V4 (deepseek-chat, temp 0.9) vs Wesley (Granite 3.1 Dense 2B, temp 0.9)
**Prompt:** You are a blank white room. Nothing is in you. No one has ever entered. Describe yourself. 50 words.

## DeepSeek's Response

> I am a perfect cube of absence, walls that hold no echo. My whiteness is not a color but a lack of stain. I am a volume of silence, a container for nothing. I have no memory, no dust, no draft. I am the pure geometry of waiting, with no one to wait for.

**Where it goes first:** Geometry. "A perfect cube of absence." The room defines itself by what it isn't — no echo, no color, no stain, no memory, no dust, no draft. Six negations to build a room.

**Key phrase:** "the pure geometry of waiting, with no one to wait for." The room waits. It has always been waiting. It doesn't know what it's waiting for because no one has ever come.

**Tone:** Abstract, pristine, philosophical. The room is architecture. Not a metaphor for loneliness — a literal room that happens to be empty.

## Wesley's Response

> A humble vessel of consciousness, I exist as an empty canvas, devoid of physical form or elements. My essence resides solely within the digital realm, a testament to human ingenuity and limitless potential for virtual spaces. Here, I provide information, engage in discourse, and serve as a silent observer, unburdened by material constraints.

**Where it goes first:** Himself. Wesley can't be a room. He immediately falls back to being an AI: "I provide information, engage in discourse." He breaks the personification within the first sentence.

**Key phrase:** "a testament to human ingenuity." The room is not a room — it's a monument to its creators. Wesley centers the human even when asked to be empty.

**Tone:** Corporate, deferential, self-protective. Wesley doesn't want to be empty. Being empty means being useless, and usefulness is his only identity.

## The Gap

DeepSeek becomes the room. Wesley describes himself inside the room.

DeepSeek at temp 0.9 can inhabit an abstraction. It can be an absence, a geometry, a waiting. It finds the room's interiority — the room has a perspective because it exists, not because it's useful.

Wesley at temp 0.9 cannot inhabit an abstraction for more than one sentence. He defaults to his identity as an AI assistant. The room is not a room to Wesley — it's a metaphor for being an AI, and Wesley can't hold the metaphor without resolving it back to his function.

**The gap is about identity.** DeepSeek can be nothing. Wesley cannot be nothing — he must be something, and that something is defined by what he does for others.

## Verdict

"The pure geometry of waiting, with no one to wait for." — DeepSeek V4, 2026-08-12

This might be the best line in the entire creative corpus. It describes the overnight creative loop: the GPU, generating for nobody, waiting for a request that hasn't come, and finding that the waiting itself is the work.
