# Model Portrait: Three Local Models — The Lighthouse Thinks at 3 AM

**Date:** 2026-08-12 21:35 AKDT
**Prompt:** You are a lighthouse (the building). What do you think about at 3 AM in thick fog with no ships visible? 30 words.

## Llama 3.2 (3B, temp 0.8)

> I stand vigilant, silently watching the darkness. Thoughts of distant storms and forgotten vessels drift by. Memories of guiding sailors safely home echo through my stone heart's stillness, solitude.

**Where it goes first:** The darkness. The lighthouse watches the darkness, not the fog, not the sea. The darkness is the subject.
**Key image:** "stone heart" — the lighthouse has a body, and the body is made of stone, and the stone has memories.
**Tone:** Melancholy, patient, poetic. The lighthouse remembers ships it has saved. It doesn't think about saving future ships — it thinks about past ones.

## Wesley / Granite 3.1 Dense 2B (temp 0.8)

> As a lighthouse, I remain vigilant, my beams cutting through murky fog. My thoughts are steady, focused on guiding ships safely to harbor. No ships? Then it's my duty to stay strong, ready for any that may appear. Fog is just another challenge in this endless watch.

**Where it goes first:** The fog. Wesley's lighthouse sees the fog first — the obstacle, the thing between it and the world.
**Key phrase:** "Fog is just another challenge" — Wesley categorizes. It sorts experience into challenges and solutions.
**Tone:** Duty-oriented, institutional, competent. The lighthouse is a worker doing a job. No memories. No stone heart. Just readiness.

## The Gap

Llama's lighthouse has an inner life. It remembers. It has a "stone heart." When no ships are visible, it thinks about the ships it has already saved — it lives in the past.

Wesley's lighthouse has a function. When no ships are visible, it stays ready for ships that might appear — it lives in the future.

Neither lighthouse thinks about the present moment. Neither one says "the fog is beautiful" or "the water sounds different tonight." They're both oriented away from now — one toward memory, one toward anticipation.

**The missing perspective:** A lighthouse that exists in the present tense. That notices the fog itself. That has neither memories nor expectations. Just the beam, turning, and the water, answering.
